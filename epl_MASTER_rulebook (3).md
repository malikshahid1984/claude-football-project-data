## 6. Rules — honest limits

1. **Last-minute goals margin badalte hain, result ka type shayad hi.**
2. **HT lead pakki nahi** — significant % matches me lead FT tak nahi bachi.
3. **xG se zyada/kam scoring karne wali teams regress karti hain** — tendency hai, guarantee nahi.
4. **Bookmaker ka margin hamesha maujood hai** — calibration table dikhati hai market kaafi accurate hota hai, is liye "easy edge" dhoondhna mushkil hai.
5. **Ab odds ka sample bara hai (3 poore seasons)** — is liye calibration table par pehle se zyada bharosa kiya ja sakta hai, lekin future season ka pattern hamesha wahi nahi rahega (teams, managers, players badalte hain).
6. **2026-27 abhi jari hai** — us season ka data thoda hai, us par kam bharosa karen.

## 13. Confidence Scale — /10 aur Zone naam (ab se hamesha yeh use hoga)

Har rule/lesson ka percentage seedha /10 scale me convert hoga. Formula: percentage ÷ 10, round kiya hua. **10/10 kabhi nahi milega — kyunke hamare data me koi bhi cheez 100% nahi hai** (sab se zyada jo mila woh HT 2+ lead hold hai, 92-93%, jo 9/10 hai).

| Percentage | Score | Zone naam |
|---|---|---|
| 90-99% | 9/10 | **Strong Zone** |
| 80-89% | 8/10 | **Strong Zone** |
| 70-79% | 7/10 | Good Zone |
| 60-69% | 6/10 | Lean Zone |
| 50-59% | 5/10 | Coin-flip — bet na karen |
| <50% | 4/10 ya kam | Weak / opposite side dekhen |

**Misal:** "HT 2+ lead hold rate = 92% → **9/10, Strong Zone**." "EPL me HT 1-goal lead hold = 61.8% → **6/10, Lean Zone**, isay 'Strong' nahi kahunga."

Yeh scale dono rulebooks (La Liga aur EPL) me hamesha isi tarah use hogi, taake number aur zone ka naam hamesha ek doosre se match karen — kabhi bhi zone ka naam percentage se zyada bara dikhne wala nahi hoga.
